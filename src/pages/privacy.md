# Invisible Sloth Website Privacy Policy

## The short version

This website is a simple informational site. Two information flows matter:

1. **If you email us,** we receive what you choose to send.
2. **When your browser loads the site,** GitHub, our host, receives ordinary technical request information needed to deliver it.

The rest of this policy explains those two things. We do not use website accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies in our site code. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track visitors across unrelated websites.

---

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how Invisible Sloth, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information on the public website at [invisiblesloth.com](https://invisiblesloth.com), including website pages that describe our products. It does not govern information handled inside any app, game, beta, software product, or other service we provide. Those products may have their own privacy policies. For example, the Tracer iOS app has a separate [privacy policy](/tracer/privacy).

This policy also does not cover third-party websites, app stores, beta-distribution platforms, or other services linked from or used in connection with our website or products. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

---

## 2. Information you send us and how we use it

The website includes email links for contact, support, or feedback. If you email us at the address shown on the relevant page, we receive the information you choose to include, such as:

- Your email address
- Your name, if included
- The content of your message
- Any attachments or technical details you choose to send

We may use or disclose information we receive directly, such as email correspondence, only when reasonably necessary to:

- Respond to and manage messages you send us
- Maintain appropriate business records and website operations
- Work with service providers acting on our behalf
- Comply with law, legal process, or enforceable government requests
- Protect our rights, safety, or security, or those of website visitors or others

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

---

## 3. What happens when you visit the site

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, image, stylesheet, or other file, GitHub and the networks that deliver the site receive ordinary technical request information:

- Your IP address
- Browser and device information
- The requested page or file
- The referring site or page
- The date and time of the request
- Error, availability, or security information

This is how the site reaches you. GitHub receives this information directly and may process it for the purposes described in its <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">privacy statement</a>, including providing, securing, troubleshooting, and improving its services.

Font files are served from our domain with the rest of the site, so loading them does not contact a separate font provider.

Outside `/storybook`, our website code does not set or read cookies and does not use browser local storage or session storage. The developer component previews at `/storybook` use local storage and session storage for interface preferences and temporary session state. We do not use that browser storage to identify or track visitors.

Other than the ordinary technical requests sent directly to GitHub to load the site, we do not knowingly allow third parties to collect personal information through the website about visitors' activities over time and across unrelated websites. Because our code does not use cross-site tracking or targeted-advertising technologies, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change how the website operates.

## 4. Retention, privacy requests, and security

If you contact us by email, we may retain your message for as long as needed to respond, keep business records, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations.

GitHub may retain technical information it receives according to its <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">privacy statement</a>. The networks GitHub uses to deliver the site may retain information according to their own policies and practices.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights concerning personal information we hold about you. You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold, request a correction, or request deletion. We will respond as required by applicable law. We may need to retain limited information where required for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.

We use reasonable measures to operate the website securely. However, no website, email system, or method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

Please keep that in mind when choosing what information to send by email.

---

## 5. Children's privacy

This website is a general-audience website and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

If we add analytics, cookies, hosted forms, advertising, accounts, or anything else that changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and describe the change in the changelog below.
