## Tracer Plus

Tracer is free to use. Tracer Plus is an optional one-time purchase within Tracer for iPhone. It is not a subscription.

Your purchase includes every Tracer Plus feature available today and every one we add later, at no extra cost, for as long as Tracer is available. Whatever price you paid, you get the same Tracer Plus features as anyone who buys later. If we ever offer Tracer Plus as a subscription, your one-time purchase still includes every Tracer Plus feature offered through that subscription.

If we stop updating Tracer or remove it from the App Store, that alone does not take away your Tracer Plus purchase. You can keep using an installed copy and its purchased features as long as Apple's applicable terms allow it and your device remains compatible. We cannot promise ongoing updates or support, compatibility with future operating-system versions, or that Tracer will remain available to download or reinstall.

Tracer Plus supports Family Sharing. Family Sharing shares the purchase, not your records.

If your purchase is refunded or Family Sharing ends, Plus features become unavailable unless you still have Tracer Plus through another purchase or family member. Your records are not changed or removed, and you can keep using all your existing active activities. The Free limit applies when you create activities or restore archived activities.

Find Restore Purchases on the Tracer Plus screen in Settings. It restores Tracer Plus, not your records. To restore your records, import a backup you exported earlier. Apple handles payments and refunds under its own terms. You can request a refund through <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Apple's Report a Problem service</a>; Apple determines eligibility.
